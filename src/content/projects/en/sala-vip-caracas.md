For the VIP lounge of Caracas international airport, an interior design project was developed for about 172 m², from the furniture layout to the choice of floors, colours and lighting.

The space is organised into **zones with different functions**: waiting seats along the glass wall, a relaxation area with modular sofas, the piano bar with round tables, a separate meeting room and a children's area near the entrance. The new walls house the bathrooms with showers and the luggage storage.

The surfaces are designed for a place of heavy traffic: hard-wearing wood-effect floors, a plasterboard false ceiling with warm LED light and light walls with deep blue accents. The furniture was coordinated with the suppliers up to production.
