In Castana, on the hills of the Oltrepò Pavese, a house left unfinished for a long time has become a warm, bright home. The starting point was a hand-drawn survey: irregular walls, fragmented rooms and a dark living area.

The new layout creates **two bedrooms with a shared bathroom** and opens the common areas into a single space, where the loft-style kitchen meets the living room. The surfaces are designed to last and age well: brick-effect strips on the main wall and around the fireplace, wood-effect stoneware in the living area and light stone finishes in the bathrooms.

The plasterboard false ceiling follows the existing volumes and hides the accent lighting, which draws the brick wall and warms the room in the evening. The project was followed from the survey to the renders, through to the choice of furniture and accessories.
