The proposal for this Milan apartment starts from a simple question: how do you really make use of every square metre? The answer is a **new interior layout**. A dedicated corridor serves the bedrooms, the wall axes are aligned and the space gained houses a second windowless bathroom.

For the interiors we followed the *less is more* principle: a clean design with a few warm touches that make the home unique and welcoming. Exposed brick, wood and black metal profiles return in every room. A slim-framed glass partition separates the TV room from the living area without taking away light.

The project includes a moodboard, the original and proposed plans, a furniture plan and 3D views of the main rooms.
